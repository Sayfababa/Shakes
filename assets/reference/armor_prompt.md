# Prompt: Rüstungs-Sheet (4×4, 1024×1024)

Mitschicken: `armor_layout.png` (Anordnung + Form) und ein Klassen- oder NPC-Portrait (Stil).

```
Create ONE image, 1024x1024 px: a 4x4 grid of 16 inventory item icons for a dark fantasy RPG, each item alone in its own 256x256 cell.
Style: hand-painted inventory art in the spirit of Diablo II item icons, painterly, gritty, worn and battle-damaged, scratches, rust,
dirt, dried blood, stitched leather, dull tarnished metal, small specular highlights, dramatic light from the upper left.
Seen straight from the front, slight 3/4 tilt allowed. Background of every cell: flat solid pure black (#000000), no floor, no shadow
on the background, no glow, no frame, no grid lines, no text, no numbers.
Keep each item centered and inside the dashed outline of the attached layout, keeping its proportions (tall items stay tall, belts stay wide).
Use the attached layout only for position and shape. Do not copy its flat vector look and do not render any labels.

Row 1: 1 leather skullcap, 2 iron great helm with eye slit and rivets, 3 bone death mask shaped like a skull face, 4 laced leather jerkin.
Row 2: 5 chainmail shirt, 6 steel breastplate with ridged plates, 7 worn leather gloves, 8 articulated steel gauntlets.
Row 3: 9 tall leather boots, 10 plated war greaves, 11 frayed dark red cloth sash, 12 thick leather belt with a heavy iron buckle.
Row 4: 13 round wooden buckler with iron rim and boss, 14 tall heater shield painted dark red with a faded gold cross,
       15 tarnished gold amulet with a dark gemstone, 16 heavy signet ring with a small dark gemstone.
Consistent lighting, scale and color grading across all 16 items. Grim, realistic, not cartoon.
```

**Negativ:** text, letters, numbers, watermark, background scene, gradient background, glow, frame, grid lines, cartoon, flat vector, 3d render, plastic, shiny new, bright colors.
