# Prompts: Monster- & Boss-Portraits

Gemeinsamer Stil-Block (in jeden Prompt): siehe unten. Mitschicken: Layout-Bild + NPC-Sheet als Stilvorlage.

## Sheet A (monster_layout_a.png, 3×2, 1536×1024)
```
Create ONE image, 1536x1024 px, a 3x2 grid of six square monster portraits (each exactly 512x512, no gaps, no borders, no text).
Match the style of the attached NPC portrait sheet exactly: painterly dark-fantasy horror realism like Diablo II / Diablo IV key art,
gritty, wet, rotten, scars and filth, chest-up or head-and-shoulders, facing the viewer aggressively, dramatic low-key light from one side,
dark brown-black smoky background, muted desaturated palette with sickly accents. Terrifying, not cartoon, not cute.
Use the attached layout only for the position of each creature. Do not render any labels or letters.
1 ROTTING GHOUL: emaciated grey-green corpse, torn lips, jagged yellow teeth, milky eyes, strips of flesh.
2 THE FALLEN: small red-skinned demon imp, horns, glowing amber eyes, crude bone fetish, snarling.
3 SKELETON WARRIOR: blackened skeleton in a rusted dented helm and torn chainmail, empty eye sockets with faint embers.
4 GRAVE ROBBER: hunched man in a filthy hooded cloak, face hidden in shadow except two glinting eyes, holding a lantern and a crowbar.
5 PLAGUE RAT: enormous diseased rat, mangy fur, open sores, red eyes, yellow teeth, in a sewer.
6 CULTIST OF THE BELL: hooded zealot in dark red robes, face covered by an iron mask shaped like a bell, small bells on chains.
Consistent lighting and color grading across all six.
```

## Bosse (boss_layout.png, 2×2, 1024×1024)
```
Create ONE image, 1024x1024 px, a 2x2 grid of four square boss portraits (each 512x512, no gaps, no borders, no text).
Same style as the attached sheets, but larger than life, more detail, more menace, a hint of an environment behind each.
1 MOTHER GERLINDE, THE DROWNED: drowned washerwoman, waterlogged pale skin, black water dripping, long wet hair over the face,
  tangled in laundry sheets and ropes, glowing white eyes.
2 BROTHER ALDEMAR, THE PLAGUED: corrupted monk swollen with plague, boils, rotten habit, swarm of flies, rosary of finger bones.
3 THE BUTCHER OF VHAL: huge brute with a cleaver, bloody leather apron, iron mask, meat hooks and chains behind him.
4 THE BELL-RINGER: towering hunchbacked figure under a giant cracked bronze bell, face of fused bone and iron, ropes and chains, ghostly sound waves of dust.
```

**Negativ:** text, letters, watermark, logo, frame, border, cartoon, anime, cute, 3d render, plastic, bright colors, clean.
