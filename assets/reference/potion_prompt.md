Mitschicken: `potion_layout.png` (Anordnung, Felder 1–20) und ein Item-Bild aus `assets/items/` (Stil).
Felder 1–14 Tränke (Flasche), 15–20 Zutaten. Zuschnitt später wie bei den Items: Schwarz → transparent, Ablage `assets/potions/<id>.webp`.

```
Create ONE image, 1024x1280 px: a grid of 4 columns x 5 rows = 20 separate inventory item paintings for a dark fantasy RPG,
each object centered in its own cell of the attached layout, with generous empty space around it.
Pure black background (#000000) everywhere, no floor, no table, no shadows on the ground, no frames, no cell borders, no grid lines.
Style: hand-painted item art in the spirit of Diablo II inventory items, painterly, gritty, dark and muted, worn and old,

No text, no letters, no labels, no runes that look like letters.
All 14 potions share ONE family look: hand-blown old glass, cork or wax stopper, leather cord, slightly dirty glass,
liquid color is the main way to tell them apart. Bottle SHAPES vary a little per row. Rare potions (7-14) have small
tarnished metal fittings (silver or bronze cage, chain) to look more precious than the common ones (1-6).
```
