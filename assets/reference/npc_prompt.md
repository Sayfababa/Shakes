# Prompt: NPC-Portraits (Sheet 3×2)

Schicke mit: 1) das Original-Klassenbild (Stil), 2) `npc_layout.png` (Anordnung).

```
Create ONE image, 1536x1024 px, a 3x2 grid of six square character portraits (each exactly 512x512, no gaps, no borders, no text).
Match the style of the attached class-portrait sheet exactly: painterly dark-fantasy realism like Diablo II / Diablo IV key art,
gritty, dirty skin, scars, worn materials, chest-up portraits, three-quarter view, dramatic low-key lighting from one side,
dark brown-black smoky studio background, muted desaturated palette with small accents. Grim and serious, not heroic or cute.
Use the attached layout image only for the position of each character. Do not render the labels, numbers or any letters.

1 (top left) HALVAR, the blacksmith: old broad-shouldered man, 60s, bald with burn scars, grey braided beard, soot-blackened leather apron
  over bare scarred arms, holding a heavy forging hammer, faint orange glow of a forge from below.
2 (top middle) MOTHER YSOLDE, the herb witch: very old gaunt woman, deep wrinkles, milky pale green eyes, wild grey hair with dried herbs
  and small bones woven in, ragged dark green-brown shawl, necklace of teeth and tiny vials, a crooked wooden pipe.
3 (top right) BROTHER KASPAR, the gravedigger monk: lean middle-aged man, hollow cheeks, stubble, wide-brimmed battered hat,
  rough brown monk's habit with rope belt, muddy hands, leaning on a spade, a small wooden holy symbol, tired haunted eyes.
4 (bottom left) VARGO, the pit master: huge brutal man, shaved head, horned iron half-helm, missing ear, chain and leather harness,
  coiled whip over the shoulder, cruel grin, fresh blood on knuckles, torchlight.
5 (bottom middle) SISTER ILSE, the crypt warden: young severe nun, pale face with ash cross on the forehead, black and grey habit,
  iron keys on her belt, raising an old brass lantern that lights her face warmly, determined cold stare.
6 (bottom right) THE BEGGAR PROPHET: emaciated old man, blindfold of dirty cloth over his eyes, long matted white beard, rags and sackcloth,
  bell on a cord around his neck, gnarled walking staff, mouth half open as if speaking a prophecy.

Each portrait centered in its cell, head in the upper third, consistent lighting and color grading across all six.
```

**Negativ (falls möglich):** text, letters, watermark, logo, frame, border, cartoon, anime, 3d render, plastic skin, bright colors, smooth, glossy, extra fingers, cropped head.

Nach dem Generieren: Bild als `assets/npcs.png` ins Repo legen (oder hier schicken) → wird in 6 Portraits zerschnitten.
