# Prompt: Zauber-Symbole (2 Sheets à 3×3, je 1024×1024)

Mitschicken: `spell_layout.png` (Anordnung) und ein Klassen-Portrait (Stil). Pro Sheet den Block A bzw. B einsetzen.

```
Create ONE image, 1024x1024 px: a 3x3 grid of 9 square skill icons for a dark fantasy RPG, each icon filling its own square cell
of the attached layout edge to edge (full bleed, no border, no frame, no rounded corners), with a thin black gap between cells.
Style: hand-painted skill icons in the spirit of Diablo II skill icons, painterly, gritty, dark and muted, strong silhouette in the middle,
dramatic light, deep black shadows, each icon readable at small size. One clear symbol per icon, not a scene.
Dark background inside every icon (near-black, slight texture). No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no grid lines.
Use the attached layout only for positions. Do not copy its flat look and do not render the numbers.
Consistent color grading across all 9 icons; each row has its own accent color as described.

[BLOCK A oder B hier einfügen]

Grim, realistic, not cartoon, not glossy.
```

**Block A (Sheet 1):**
```
Row 1 (barbarian, accent dark red and iron):
1 a screaming bearded warrior's face in profile with visible breath and sound waves of dust,
2 a spinning heavy axe blade leaving a circular motion trail,
3 a blood-red drop with a cracked iron fang inside it, dripping.
Row 2 (paladin, accent pale gold and white):
4 a sword striking down wrapped in pale holy light,
5 two gauntleted hands pressed together in prayer with faint golden glow,
6 a glowing war hammer falling from above with a radiant halo.
Row 3 (assassin, accent violet and black):
7 a curved dagger emerging from black smoke,
8 a dagger blade dripping green poison,
9 a burning violet skull-shaped mark branded on dark skin.
```

**Block B (Sheet 2):**
```
Row 1 (huntress, accent green and brown):
1 an arrow on a drawn bowstring seen from the archer's eye, a small red target point,
2 many arrows raining down from a dark sky,
3 a rusty iron bear trap snapping shut.
Row 2 (sorceress, accent fire orange and icy blue):
4 a roaring fireball,
5 a ring of jagged ice shards bursting outward,
6 a flaming meteor falling with a long fire trail.
Row 3 (grave witch, accent sickly green and bone):
7 a sharp bone spear flying with green spectral trail,
8 a cloud of green plague mist with a faint skull shape in it,
9 a wall of stacked bones and skulls with green glow between them.
```

**Negativ:** text, letters, numbers, watermark, frame, border, rounded corners, grid lines, cartoon, flat vector, 3d render, plastic, glossy, bright saturated colors, characters' full bodies.

Danach: Bild hier schicken → wird in 9 Symbole zerschnitten und den Zaubern zugeordnet (Zeile = Klasse, Spalte = Stufe 1/8/18).
